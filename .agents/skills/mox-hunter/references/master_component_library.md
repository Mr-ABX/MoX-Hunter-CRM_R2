# 💎 Master UI Component Library & Blueprint Reference
*(Awwwards, Siteinspire, Mobbin & One Page Love Benchmarks)*

Every prototype built by `mox-ui-designer` must select a **completely unique combination** of Header, Hero, Form/CTA, and Footer from this master reference. No two websites in a campaign can share the same component blueprint.

---

## 🧭 1. The 5 Distinct Header & Navbar Blueprints

### Header Blueprint A: Floating Glassmorphism Island (e.g. Modern Clinical)
* **Visual**: Detached pill floating 20px from top, frosted glass `backdrop-blur-md bg-slate-900/80 border border-slate-700/60 rounded-full px-6 py-3.5 shadow-2xl`.
* **Left**: Real Clinic Logo + "F-7 Markaz" glowing status dot (`bg-emerald-400 animate-pulse`).
* **Center**: Minimalist links (Procedures, Before/After, Reviews, Doctor Faculty).
* **Right**: Emergency/Direct Phone Pill + Gradient "Book Appointment" CTA button.

### Header Blueprint B: Dual-Tier British Royal Header (e.g. Harley Street / Kensington)
* **Top Bar**: Oxford Blue `#0b1426` with British Standard badge 🇬🇧, working hours (`Mon–Sat: 9am–8pm`), direct clinic telephone with telephone icon.
* **Main Bar**: Sticky `#0b1120/95` border-b border-amber-500/20 with large Kensington Logo, doctor qualifications (`GDC Registered / UK Faculty`), and Gold Foil Consultation CTA.

### Header Blueprint C: Ultra-Luxury Asymmetrical Monogram (e.g. Beverly Centre / Hissam)
* **Visual**: Deep Obsidian Black `#050a12`, large gold monogram crest on left (`HA`), centered serif brand name in `Italiana`, right-side VIP Concierge phone with pulsing gold ring.

### Header Blueprint D: Medical Specialist Ribbon (e.g. Jinnah Super / Dental Consultants)
* **Visual**: Clean clinical emerald & titanium palette with direct address badge (`Office #8, Block 12-C, Jinnah Super Market`), Class-B sterilization guarantee pill, and WhatsApp direct trigger.

### Header Blueprint E: 24/7 Emergency Alert Header (e.g. Smile Square)
* **Top Bar**: High-contrast flashing red/amber alert strip: *"🚨 24/7 Emergency Dental Surgery on Call in F-7 Markaz • Same-Day Pain Relief"*.
* **Main Bar**: High-tech cobalt blue with bold emergency hotline `(0333) 555-6789` and instant WhatsApp triage action.
