# Dynamic Layout Rhythm & Anti-Twin Architecture Standards

To guarantee that no two prototypes in MoX Hunter ever look alike or feel like generic AI templates, every prototype synthesized must strictly adopt one of the following distinct layout structures:

---

## 🎨 The 5 Unique Structural Archetypes

### 1. Archetype 1: Clinical Modern & Before/After (e.g. Care N Cure)
