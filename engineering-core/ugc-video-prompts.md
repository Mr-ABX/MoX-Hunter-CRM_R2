# UGC Video Testimonial Prompts for Gemini Omni Flash / Veo 3

Use these highly detailed prompts in your video generation flow to create realistic, 10-second selfie-style testimonials for the MO-X Hunter landing page.

## Prompt 1: HVAC / Home Services Owner
**Prompt:** A 10-second realistic selfie-style UGC video of a rugged middle-aged man in a dark blue work shirt, standing in front of a slightly out-of-focus white HVAC service van parked in a suburban driveway. He is holding the phone with one hand, looking directly into the camera with a relieved, confident smile. The lighting is natural, slightly overcast daylight. The environment feels like an authentic mid-workday break. He speaks enthusiastically with natural hand gestures, conveying how his new AI-built website has his phone ringing off the hook.

## Prompt 2: Local Bakery Owner
**Prompt:** A 10-second cinematic but authentic smartphone video of a woman in her 30s wearing a flour-dusted canvas apron, standing inside a warm, softly lit local bakery. Fresh artisan bread and pastries are visible on wooden shelves in the blurred background. She is holding the phone, smiling brightly into the lens. The lighting is warm tungsten mixed with natural window light. She gestures with her free hand, visibly thrilled, explaining how the automated marketing brought in a flood of new local customers in just 24 hours.

## Prompt 3: Auto Repair Shop Owner
**Prompt:** A 10-second gritty, authentic UGC selfie video of a man in his 40s wearing a dark grey mechanic's work shirt with a name patch, standing inside a busy auto repair garage. A car raised on a hydraulic lift is visible in the background. The lighting is a mix of overhead fluorescent and natural light from a large open garage door. He has a slight smudge of grease on his cheek, nodding confidently at the camera, talking excitedly about how the automated social media completely replaced his expensive marketing agency.
